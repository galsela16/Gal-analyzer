GAL Analyzer — Privacy Policy
Updated: October 9, 2026

This policy applies to the GAL Analyzer desktop application.

Audio processing
The app accesses the microphone or selected audio interface only after you start
audio input and grant macOS microphone permission. Audio is processed locally for
measurements. Starting input can activate every input channel of the selected
interface; MIC and REF routing selects the channels used for pair measurements.
Stopping input or closing the app stops capture. The app does not upload audio.

Local storage and exports
Settings, calibration data and captured traces are stored in the app's local
profile on your Mac. Session, CSV and image exports are saved to files you choose.
Files you import are processed locally. Exported files may include measurements,
trace names, calibration data and timestamps. You control any subsequent sharing.
Clear all removes captured traces from the current collection; previously exported
files remain on disk until you delete them. macOS backups may include local files.

Data collection and tracking
The app has no advertising, tracking, account system, remote analytics or cloud
storage. It does not send measurements or usage information to the developer.
Remote HTTP and HTTPS requests from the measurement window are blocked. Electron's
crash reporter is not enabled. Apple may separately process store purchases,
downloads or diagnostic data under Apple's own privacy policy.

Support
If you choose to contact galanalyzer@gmail.com, your message and any attachments
are shared with the developer and handled by the email services used to send and
receive them. Send only the information needed for support. If you report an issue
through GitHub, GitHub processes that submission under its own policies. Do not
include private recordings or personal information in public issues. The app does
not automatically send support messages or reports. Support contact and setup help:
https://github.com/galsela16/Gal-analyzer/blob/main/desktop/store/SUPPORT.md

The application is provided by the maintainer of that repository. Any change to
these practices will be reflected in this policy in a future app release.
