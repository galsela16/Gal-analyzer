# GAL Analyzer 5.5.59

- TF Trace Capture now asks for Pink Noise, Sweep or an external source before saving.
- Pink Noise and Sweep start automatically, settle long enough for capture, then stop automatically when they were not already running.
- Sweep capture waits for the complete one-shot sweep before storing the trace.
- External-source capture leaves playback under the operator's control and captures after a short settling period.
- Verified and Unverified trace labeling remains based on the actual TF quality at capture time.
- The large TF trust notice is replaced by a compact one-line corner badge.
- The permanent Generator rail card now has a synchronized direct start/stop button above Open Generator.
